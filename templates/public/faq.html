FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Is there really a catch?

No. We're a volunteer group — local students who already had the equipment and wanted something better to do with a Saturday. Nobody is going to hand you an invoice or try to sell you a maintenance plan afterwards.

Who actually shows up?

Someone from our crew — all local, mostly high school and college students. You get their name and number before the day, and it's the same person start to finish.

How do you pick a time?

Everyone sets the hours they're free to volunteer. When your request comes in we check who's open on the day you asked for, who isn't already booked, and who's had the lightest week, then offer you that slot. If nothing fits we send you the next few openings instead.

Do I need to be home?

Not for outside work, as long as we can reach an outdoor spigot and the gate is open. Put the code or the dog in the notes on the form.

What about my plants?

We wet them down before and rinse them after. Point out anything delicate and we'll tarp it.

What if it rains?

We text the night before and move you to the next open slot. Rescheduling is never a problem — we'd rather do it properly.

Are you insured?

We carry general liability through our parents' policy for residential work in our service area. Ask and we'll send the certificate.

My town isn't on the list.

Call anyway — {{ BUSINESS.phone }}. If it's a short drive and you're flexible on the day, we can usually make it work.

Can I join the crew?

Yes, and we need people. You don't need experience — you need a free afternoon now and then and to not mind getting soaked. Email {{ BUSINESS.email }} and tell us which days you're around.
